# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

PRDSprint is intended to be a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

Expected behavior includes:

- Communicating respectfully and constructively
- Assuming good intent while addressing impact
- Giving and receiving feedback professionally
- Focusing discussion on the work and the needs of the community

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, sexualized conduct, publishing private information without permission, and other conduct inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to [ayushrock3006@gmail.com](mailto:ayushrock3006@gmail.com). Reports will be reviewed promptly and fairly. Maintainers may remove content, close discussions, or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html).
