# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 2026-08-07

This Privacy Policy explains how PRDSprint (“PRDSprint”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) may handle information when you use the application or project services. This template is intended for the initial public release and should be reviewed for the final operating entity, jurisdiction, retention periods, and contact details before a commercial launch.

## Information we may process

- **Account information:** email address, authentication-provider identifiers, and profile information supplied through Supabase Auth.
- **Project content:** product descriptions, builder selections, style choices, generated PRD sections, support messages, and related metadata that you submit.
- **Usage and billing records:** credit balances, generation status, payment records, refunds, plan information, and audit events where those backend systems are enabled.
- **Technical information:** browser, device, network, error, and access information needed to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the service.

Do not submit passwords, API keys, payment card numbers, health information, or other sensitive information into project descriptions or support forms unless the service explicitly requests and protects it.

## How information is used

We may use information to authenticate users, provide PRD generation and exports, manage credits and plans, respond to support requests, administer the service, prevent abuse, investigate security incidents, improve reliability, and meet legal obligations.

## AI providers and service providers

When generation is enabled, submitted generation inputs may be processed by the server-side AI provider configured for the deployment. The specific provider, retention, and transfer terms depend on the deployed Edge Function configuration. Supabase may process authentication, database, function, and storage data under its own terms. Review provider policies before using production or confidential data.

## Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared with infrastructure, authentication, hosting, storage, analytics, payment, support, and AI providers only as needed to operate the configured service, with administrators when necessary for support or security, or when required by law.

## Retention and security

Retention depends on the deployed Supabase schema and operational policy. We apply reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards, but no internet service is completely secure. Access controls, Row Level Security, secret management, backups, and retention schedules must be configured for the deployment.
